# Tableau – Hospital Performance, Quality & Efficiency Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

This module presents an interactive business intelligence analysis of FY2026 hospital performance using the consolidated CMS hospital dataset containing 2,455 hospitals.

Tableau was used to explore hospital-level and state-level performance patterns, compare weighted quality-domain scores, identify performance gaps, and investigate variations in hospital quality and efficiency.

The analysis was structured around eight business questions and presented through four interactive dashboards.

## 2. Analytical Objectives

- Examine the distribution of hospital performance across the Total Performance Score (TPS).
- Compare weighted scores across Clinical Outcomes, Person and Community Engagement, Safety, and Efficiency.
- Evaluate differences in hospital performance across states.
- Identify the strongest and weakest quality domains.
- Investigate consistency and variation in hospital quality-domain performance.
- Identify hospitals with notable performance gaps across quality domains.

## 3. Dashboard Development

Four interactive dashboards were developed to communicate the findings.

### Dashboard 1 – Hospital Performance Overview

Focused on understanding the overall distribution and variation of hospital performance using Total Performance Scores.

**Analytical focus:**
- Overall hospital performance distribution.
- TPS-based performance comparisons.
- Identification of performance patterns across hospitals.

### Dashboard 2 – Quality Domain Consistency & Gaps

*An Analysis of Hospital Quality Strengths, Weaknesses and Performance Mismatches.*

Examined differences among the four weighted quality-domain scores to identify hospital-level strengths, weaknesses, and inconsistencies.

**Analytical focus:**
- Comparison of weighted quality-domain performance.
- Identification of domain-level strengths and weaknesses.
- Analysis of performance mismatches across domains.
- Examination of consistency in hospital quality performance.

### Dashboard 3 – State-wise Hospital Performance Analysis

Focused on geographical variations in hospital performance and comparisons across states.

**Analytical focus:**
- State-wise hospital performance.
- Comparison of average weighted domain scores.
- Identification of variations in quality and efficiency across states.

### Dashboard 4 – Hospital Quality & Efficiency Insights

Explored additional hospital-level performance patterns to provide a deeper understanding of quality-domain differences and efficiency-related outcomes.

**Analytical focus:**
- Comparative quality and efficiency analysis.
- Identification of performance gaps.
- Exploration of hospital-level variations.

## 4. Tableau Techniques Applied

- Parameters for interactive analysis.

- Dashboard Actions for interactive navigation and filtering.
